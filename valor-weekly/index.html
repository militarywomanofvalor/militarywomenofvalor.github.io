From Our Founder

Welcome to The Valor Weekly

By Lena Goodman

Dear Sister Veterans,

Welcome to The Valor Weekly, I am truly glad you are here.

I founded Military Women of Valor because of the women in my own family, my daughter served eight years in the United States Navy and three other female members of my family also served. I watched how hard the road home can be once the uniform comes off, a woman coming home deserves a safe place to live, care for her mind and heart and the respect she earned through her service. Those needs became my life's work.

The Valor Weekly is our way of staying close, every Monday you will find the news that matters to women veterans along with events near you, career opportunities and practical tools that make life a little easier.

You will also find something just for you, caring for yourself is part of our mission and every issue will celebrate self care, wellness, beauty, fashion, music, travel and sisterhood.

You do not need to have everything figured out to reach out. You do not need to be in crisis to deserve support. You earned your benefits the day you raised your hand to serve and using them takes nothing from anyone.

This space was created for you, if there is a story you would love to read or a topic you would like us to explore please send your suggestion to contact@militarywomenofvalor.org. Every suggestion will be thoughtfully considered, if we feature your idea you will hear from us within 7 to 14 days.

With gratitude and love,

Lena Goodman

Founder · Military Women of Valor

Who We Are

Welcome to Military Women of Valor

Military Women of Valor is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that honors, supports and uplifts the women who have worn our nation's uniform, whether you served four years or forty you belong here.

Our Vision

A future where every female veteran has access to safe affordable housing and the supportive services she needs.

Our Mission

To honor the service and sacrifice of female veterans by providing safe transitional housing options, supportive service referrals and pathways to permanent housing and lasting success.

What We Do Today

Today we serve as a trusted resource hub, while we do not yet operate housing we connect women veterans with the people and programs ready to help so no one has to search alone.

  • Housing referrals to trusted shelters, transitional homes and housing programs from New York to Virginia
  • A direct connection to the VA for health care, mental health support, benefits and women's health services
  • Weekly listings of veteran job fairs, hiring events and women veterans gatherings
  • A resource directory and archive available at any time

Where We Are Going

Housing is the heart of our work, our dream is a home of our own where women veterans can rest, heal and plan their next chapter surrounded by support. We are actively seeking the funding and partners to make that dream a reality.

How You Can Help

  • Share this newsletter with a woman veteran who could use it
  • Volunteer your time or talents
  • Partner with us if your organization serves veterans or provides housing
  • Give a gift of any size toward the home we are building

Reach us at contact@militarywomenofvalor.org.

For You This Fall

The Season of Caring for Yourself

Shorter days are an invitation to slow down, this week we gathered small comforts for the mind, the body and the spirit.

Self Care

The Ten Minute Fall Reset

Give yourself ten quiet minutes each evening this week and treat them as an appointment with yourself.

  1. Dim the lights and silence your phone.
  2. Sit with both feet flat on the floor.
  3. Breathe in through your nose for a count of four.
  4. Breathe out through your mouth for a count of six.
  5. Repeat five times and feel your shoulders soften.

Wellness

Walk Among the Leaves

A twenty minute walk in the crisp fall air can lift your mood and quiet your mind, bring a friend or savor the solitude.

As you walk notice five things you can see, four things you can hear and three things you can smell, it is a gentle way to return to the present moment.

For support with movement and healthy eating ask your local VA medical center about its free weight management program.

Healthy Food

Roasted Butternut Squash Soup

Warm, simple and affordable this golden soup serves four and makes a week of easy lunches.

You Will Need
  • 1 butternut squash
  • 1 yellow onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cups low sodium vegetable broth
  • A pinch of cinnamon with salt and pepper to taste
Method
  1. Heat the oven to 400 degrees.
  2. Peel and cube the squash, toss it with half the oil and roast for 35 to 40 minutes until tender.
  3. Cook the chopped onion and garlic in the remaining oil until soft.
  4. Add the squash and broth and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Blend until smooth and season with cinnamon, salt and pepper.

Beauty

Beauty by Rackelle

Rackelle · Veteran Aesthetician

Meet Rackelle, a veteran and aesthetician who will share the skin and beauty rituals that help you glow from the inside out.

As the air turns cool and dry switch to a richer moisturizer at night and keep a nourishing lip balm within reach.

Wear sunscreen every morning because the autumn sun still reaches your skin, a glass of water with every meal will keep your complexion luminous all season.

Fashion

Confidence in Layers

Fall is the season of beautiful layers, three pieces will carry you from morning errands to an evening out.

  • A well fitted coat in camel or navy
  • A soft knit sweater in a warm neutral
  • A pair of comfortable ankle boots

Add a scarf in a jewel tone such as burgundy or emerald to bring warmth to your face, above all wear what makes you stand tall.

Travel

A Day of Honor

Plan a day trip to the Military Women's Memorial at the gateway to Arlington National Cemetery, women who served can register their service there and add their story to the national record.

Veterans may also receive a free lifetime pass to national parks and federal recreation lands through the America the Beautiful program, fall color makes this the perfect season to use it.

Sisterhood

Find Your People

No one should walk the road home alone, this week reach out to one woman you served with because a short call or a handwritten note can mean more than you know.

Groups such as Military Women Across the Nation and the women veterans programs in every state host gatherings throughout the year, we will share them here every Monday.

What You Should Know

This Week's News

More Veterans Are Choosing VA Health Care

More than 300,000 veterans enrolled in VA health care in 2026 and it is never too late to apply, visit VA.gov or call 1 877 222 8387 to learn whether you qualify.

A Free Guide to PTSD Treatment

The National Center for PTSD offers a free online tool that compares proven treatments and helps you prepare questions for your provider, a special section is designed for family members.

Sleep Care From Home

VA TeleSleep connects veterans with sleep specialists by video and can send testing equipment to your home, ask your VA care team whether it is right for you.
